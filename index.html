Deal Bot

Automatic price-comparison posts for AliExpress products.

Deal Bot is a personal tool run by its owner. It finds popular AliExpress products, compares prices from different sellers, and publishes the result as an image and a short video to the owner's own social media accounts, including the owner's own YouTube channel.

The app is used only by its owner. It does not offer accounts, sign-ups or services to the public.
