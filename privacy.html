Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Deal Bot is a personal automation tool operated by its owner for their own use. It is not offered to the public and has no other users. Contact: 0slimmicco@gmail.com

What Google user data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests permission to manage the owner's own YouTube account (the YouTube Data API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube). The app uses this permission only to:

  • upload videos (YouTube Shorts) created by the app to the owner's own YouTube channel, and
  • read the name of the owner's channel to confirm that the sign-in worked.

How the data is used and stored

The app stores a single sign-in token (a refresh token) in the owner's private Google Drive so that it can keep uploading videos without asking the owner to sign in again. No other Google user data is collected, copied or stored.

Sharing

Google user data is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party. It is not used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Deal Bot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the stored token from their Google Drive.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.
